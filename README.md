In Flex 4, I calculated Line Price by hand for each individual sales line by dividing Revenue by (1 - Discount %). I would build this in Power BI as a calculated column because the calculation relies on fixed, row-level attributes on a single transaction row and needs to be evaluated independently for every item before any aggregation occurs. If Line Price lives in the Power BI model as a calculated column instead of remaining buried inside my Excel workbook, Marcus and Anita can immediately filter or group sales performance by transaction-level price tiers without waiting for manual workbook updates, giving him a self-service report that refreshes automatically whenever the central dataset updates.

** Nov 2, 2026 · Data Storytelling with Adobe Express

Work sample for Renee Castillo, Director of Card Operations at Bear Down Bank: approve early replacement batch for target non-contactless cards to accelerate the contactless rollout timeline.

Design choice: I choose a horizontal bar chart with year of expiration as my X axis and count of cards as my Y axis along with the a table showing the cost of replacement for the various card types and years. I went with this to show both how credit cards are the thing holding back contactless adoption back by 2 years and the relatively low cost of fixing this with a replacement batch. Originally there was a pie chart in there as well, but it got panned pretty hard in development and was cut. I recommended to do a replacement batch for credit cards that expire in 2029 and 2030, as previously mentinoned they hold back the timeline by 2 years and have a low cost of replacement at $240 dollars.
